# v1.45 — Verified invariant → hypothesis orchestration

CYDRA v1.45 integrates the existing verified-invariant hypothesis bridge into the reasoning orchestrator.

- Only invariants explicitly marked `verified=True` may participate in the bridge.
- Planner probability and prediction data remain caller-supplied; invariant confidence is never silently converted into planner probability.
- Verified invariant provenance is persisted as an `informs` relationship to the mapped hypothesis.
- The resulting hypothesis continues through the normal information-gain planner and `tested_by` observation chain.
- No graph mutation occurs when no authorized observation can be planned.
